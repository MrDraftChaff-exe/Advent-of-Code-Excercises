Open on the DualSense, unveiled April 7 2020 and launched with PlayStation 5 on November 12 2020 in the US, Japan, and other first-wave markets. PS5 Controllers (2020)
Image: White DualSense. Alex Cochrane, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Sony unveiled the DualSense on April 7, 2020.
It launched with the PlayStation 5 on November 12, 2020, in the US, Japan, and other first-wave markets.
It weighs about 280 grams and measures about 160 by 66 by 106 mm.
Voice-coil actuators in the grips can make wind and sand feel like they hit your hands.
Motors in the L2 and R2 triggers add resistance, like a bowstring tightening as you draw it.
Those trigger effects can be reduced or switched off.
The Share button became Create, and a built-in microphone and mute button work without a headset.
USB-C replaced micro-USB, Bluetooth 5.1 handles wireless play, and a 3.5 mm jack takes a headset.
The original DualSense has a 1,560 mAh battery that charges in about three hours from the console.
A light bar sits on the touchpad edges, and five LEDs mark your player number.
DualSense Edge launched on January 26, 2023, at $199.99, with replaceable sticks, back buttons, and adjustable triggers.
The Access controller launched on December 6, 2023, at $89.99, with four 3.5 mm ports and a layout that works at any angle.

Cheapest Versus Quality (2026)
Image: White DualSense. Alex Cochrane, CC BY-SA 4.0.

PlayStation Direct lists the DualSense at $74 on October 21, 2026.
Walmart lists a white DualSense at $66.95 the same day.
The Access controller is $89 on PlayStation Direct, and the Edge is $199 there.
Amazon lists the Edge at $189.05, five dollars under Sony's price.
Days of Play 2026 cut a DualSense to $54, and that sale is over.
Sony says PS5 games play with a DualSense, and licensed PS5 pads are the other route Sony sells.
An unlicensed bargain pad costs less, and PS5 games are built for a DualSense or a licensed PS5 controller.
The $67 DualSense keeps grip haptics, adaptive triggers, the mic, and the touchpad.
IGN's February 2026 guide names that stock pad the best overall PS5 controller.
The Edge keeps the same haptics and triggers, then adds back buttons and replaceable sticks.
IGN puts Edge battery life at about 5 to 6 hours, shorter than the cheaper controller.
Triple the price buys paddles or drift-proof sticks. The DualSense feel stays on the $67 pad.
