PS5 Controllers (2020)
Image: White DualSense. Alex Cochrane, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Sony unveiled the DualSense on April 7, 2020.
It launched with the PlayStation 5 on November 12, 2020, in the US, Japan, and other first-wave markets.
It weighs about 280 grams and measures about 160 by 66 by 106 mm.
Voice-coil actuators in the grips can make wind and sand feel like they hit your hands.
Motors in the L2 and R2 triggers add resistance, like a bowstring tightening as you draw it.
Those trigger effects can be reduced or switched off.
The Share button became Create, and a built-in microphone and mute button work without a headset.
USB-C replaced micro-USB, Bluetooth 5.1 handles wireless play, and a 3.5 mm jack takes a headset.
The original DualSense has a 1,560 mAh battery that charges in about three hours from the console.
A light bar sits on the touchpad edges, and five LEDs mark your player number.
DualSense Edge launched on January 26, 2023, at $199.99, with replaceable sticks, back buttons, and adjustable triggers.
The Access controller launched on December 6, 2023, at $89.99, with four 3.5 mm ports and a layout that works at any angle.
