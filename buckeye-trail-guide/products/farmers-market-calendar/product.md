# Central Ohio Farmers Market Seasonal Calendar

Use this to shop what’s actually good — not what’s been on a truck for a week. Hours and vendors change; confirm before you drive.

## How to use

1. Circle what’s in peak season this month.
2. Pick one market type (neighborhood · downtown · suburban).
3. Fill the weekly worksheet so you don’t overbuy.

## Seasonal peaks (Central Ohio — typical)

| Month | Usually excellent |
| --- | --- |
| April–May | Asparagus, greens, radishes, rhubarb, herbs |
| June | Strawberries, peas, early zucchini, lettuce |
| July | Blueberries, beans, cucumbers, summer squash, tomatoes start |
| August | Tomatoes, sweet corn, peppers, peaches, melons |
| September | Apples, pears, grapes, late tomatoes, squash begins |
| October | Apples, pumpkins, winter squash, root veggies, greens return |
| Nov–Mar | Storage crops, greenhouse greens, pantry staples, meat/eggs/baked goods |

## Storage quick tips

- Greens: dry, sealed, cold — wash when you use
- Berries: don’t wash until eating; cold ASAP
- Tomatoes: counter until ripe (fridge dulls flavor)
- Herbs: jar of water on the counter *or* wrapped cold — test what lasts for you
- Corn: cook same day when you can
