## Timing

| Goal | Tip |
| --- | --- |
| Photos | Arrive at dusk, not deep night |
| Less traffic | Weeknights beat Saturdays |
| Kids | One wow stop + cocoa > four rushed lots |
| Guests | Pre-assign a navigator who is not driving |

## Car pack (cold nights)

- [ ] Full fuel before the first stop
- [ ] Phone chargers
- [ ] Blanket / spare gloves
- [ ] Water + snack
- [ ] Cash/card for paid displays / tips
- [ ] Jump pack if your battery is elderly

## Comfort rules

- [ ] Bathroom stop planned before stop #1
- [ ] Agree on a hard end time
- [ ] One playlist — no DJ fights
- [ ] If a lot is chaos, use the backup — don’t “circle once more”
