# Move to Columbus — Settling Pack

For people relocating to Columbus / Franklin County (and close suburbs). Print the PDF. Track the live numbers in the spreadsheet. Ignore fluff.

## What’s in this pack

| File | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| This PDF | Readable checklists and guidance |
| `Move-to-Columbus-Settling-Spreadsheet.xlsx` | Fillable trackers with auto-totals |

### Spreadsheet tabs

1. **Start Here** — your move details  
2. **Pre-Move** — checklist with done/remaining counts  
3. **Neighborhood Score** — score 1–5 across 3 areas (totals + winner)  
4. **Week 1 Setup** — utilities, address changes, local finds  
5. **Month 1 Budget** — planned vs actual with variance  
6. **Apartment Compare** — true monthly cost (rent + fees + utilities + commute)  
7. **Day 30 Check-In** — settled score  
8. **Local Rolodex** — pharmacy, grocery, urgent care, etc.  

Yellow cells = your inputs. Green cells = calculated.

## Pre-move (2–4 weeks out)

- [ ] Confirm lease/start date and utility transfer windows
- [ ] Photo-document empty place condition on move-in day
- [ ] Set mail forwarding
- [ ] Get quotes: movers vs truck+friends
- [ ] Measure elevator / stair constraints
- [ ] Plan first-night kit (sheets, towels, toilet paper, charger, meds, snacks)
- [ ] Download: ParkColumbus (if relevant), COTA, weather, maps offline area

## Week 1 setup

- [ ] Electric / gas / water / trash accounted for
- [ ] Internet installed or hotspot backup
- [ ] Renter’s insurance binder saved offline
- [ ] Change address: bank, insurance, employer HR, subscriptions
- [ ] Ohio DL / ID update plan (if relocating from out of state)
- [ ] Vehicle registration / parking permit research for your street
- [ ] Find: pharmacy, urgent care, grocery, hardware
- [ ] Test smoke/CO detectors; buy batteries if needed
- [ ] Meet one neighbor or building manager on purpose

## Day 30 check-in

- [ ] Emergency contacts on fridge / phone favorites
- [ ] Primary care / dentist search started
- [ ] One “regular” coffee or grocery routine established
- [ ] Know your street’s overnight parking / snow rules
- [ ] Have a severe weather plan (basement / interior room)
- [ ] Social: one recurring local activity on calendar (league, meetup, class, faith, hobby)

## Packing priority for Columbus seasons

Bring sooner than you think:

- [ ] Solid rain jacket
- [ ] Ice scraper if you have a car
- [ ] Door mat (salt / mud season)
- [ ] Window covers for summer heat

## Done looks like

You can answer: where you buy food, how you get to work, what happens in a storm, and who you’d text locally if something breaks.
