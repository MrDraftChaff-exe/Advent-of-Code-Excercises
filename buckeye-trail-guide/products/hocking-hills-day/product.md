# Hocking Hills Day Trip — From Columbus

A one-day plan from Columbus to Hocking Hills without the scramble. Leave early, hike the highlights, get home before dark.

## How to use

1. Pick a **departure window** the night before (weather + traffic decide).
2. Choose **Route A** (classic parks loop) or **Route B** (fewer stops, more trail time).
3. Print this sheet or keep it offline — cell service drops in the gorge.

---

## Pre-trip checklist (night before)

- [ ] Check park alerts / trail closures
- [ ] Fill gas in Columbus; pack water (2L+ per person)
- [ ] Download offline maps for the park cluster
- [ ] Pack shoes with grip, light layers, rain shell
- [ ] Cooler snacks + cash for small lots / tips
- [ ] Tell someone your return ETA
- [ ] Decide: one carpool or two cars (parking fills early)

## Drive timing (Columbus → park cluster)

| Window | Leave Columbus | Arrive parks (approx) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early bird | 6:30–7:00 | ~8:00–8:30 | Best parking at Old Man's Cave |
| Standard | 8:00–8:30 | ~9:30–10:00 | Still workable weekdays |
| Late start | after 9:30 | after 11:00 | Expect full lots; have a backup trail |

Drive is roughly **1–1.5 hours** depending on traffic south of town. Build a 20-minute buffer.

## Route A — Classic highlights (most first-timers)

| Time | Stop | Time on site | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | Old Man's Cave / visitor area | 90–120 min | Main gorge loop; restrooms + maps |
| Mid-morning | Ash Cave | 30–45 min | Short walk; big payoff; often crowded |
| Midday | Lunch | 45 min | Picnic tables or nearby town grab |
| Afternoon | Cedar Falls *or* Rock House | 60–90 min | Pick one — don't rush both |
| 3:30–4:00 | Start return drive | — | Aim to be rolling before dusk |

**Rule:** two solid stops beat four rushed ones.

---

## Route B — Deeper hike day

| Time | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Arrive early | Old Man's Cave → Grandma Gatewood connector if open/conditions allow |
| Midday | Packed lunch on trail or at a quiet picnic area |
| Afternoon | One secondary stop only if energy remains |
| Leave by 3:30 | Same return rule |

Skip Ash Cave if lots are full and you already got gorge time.

## Weather & season swaps

| Condition | Do this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Heavy rain / flash flood risk | Stay on high/short overlooks; postpone gorge bottom walks |
| Ice / freeze-thaw | Microspikes or cancel — sandstone gets slick |
| Peak fall color weekend | Leave by 6:00am; expect traffic both ways |
| Summer heat | Start earliest window; shade breaks every hour |
| Fog / low visibility | Delay departure 30–60 min; keep headlights on |

## On-trail rules that prevent headaches

1. Stay on marked trails — sandstone edges crumble.
2. One person owns the timeline; everyone else follows.
3. Turn around with enough light and fuel for the drive home.
4. No drone / cliff-edge photos that make the group wait.
