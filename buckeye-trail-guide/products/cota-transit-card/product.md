# COTA / First-Week Transit Card

For new riders and anyone rebuilding a car-light week.  

**Not affiliated with COTA.** Fares, routes, and apps change — confirm in official tools before you go.

## First-week setup

- [ ] Install official trip-planning / fare apps you actually need
- [ ] Save home pin + work/school pin offline screenshots
- [ ] Learn your nearest stop name (not just “by the coffee shop”)
- [ ] Buy/validate fare the way you’ll use it on a busy morning (test once)
- [ ] Identify a backup: rideshare · bike · different route

## Weekday timing notes

- Build a **10–15 minute** buffer the first week
- Night and weekend frequencies often drop — check before late plans
- Weather: waterproof shoes beat clever jackets when you’re standing at a stop

## Rider habits that reduce stress

- [ ] Headphones at half volume so you hear stop announcements
- [ ] Sit where you can see stop requests / doors
- [ ] Know the last reliable trip home before you go out
- [ ] Keep a folded paper this card + charger
