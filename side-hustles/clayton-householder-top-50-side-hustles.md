# Side hustles after Compliance said no

**Status:** Wells Fargo Compliance denied outside employment / competing activity.  
**Rule going forward:** Do not start consulting, freelancing, client services, gigs-as-a-business, or anything that looks like a second job until Compliance explicitly green-lights that category in writing.

This is **not** legal advice. Use it as a clarification list to take back to Compliance — not a workaround.

---

## What “no” usually kills (drop these)

Assume these are **off the table** unless Compliance reverses:

- CyberArk / security / IT consulting or contracting  
- vCISO, mentorship-for-pay marketed as professional services  
- Running a cleaning / detailing / lawn / vending **business**  
- DoorDash / Uber / Flex / Taskrabbit as ongoing work  
- Airbnb/STR as a hosted business (often flagged as outside business)  
- Building/selling SaaS or agency services  
- Any work for banks, fintechs, security vendors, or WF competitors  

If you already asked broadly and got a blanket “no,” don’t soft-launch these under a friend’s LLC either.

---

## Ask Compliance to classify these buckets (yes / no / disclose)

Bring this exact list. You want category rulings, not vibes.

### Bucket A — Personal finance (usually not “employment”)
| Idea | Why it might still be OK | Confirm with Compliance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brokerage / index funds / HYSAs | Personal investing | Personal trading / restricted list / blackout rules |
| Employer ESPP/401k maximization | Internal benefits | Already covered by HR |
| I-bonds / Treasuries | Personal | Same as investing |
| Peer-to-peer lending / crowdfunding debt | Can look like a business at scale | Thresholds, platforms banned? |

### Bucket B — Sell your own stuff (one-time, not a store)
| Idea | Notes | Confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook Marketplace / eBay of **personal** household items | Declutter, not inventory flips | “Are occasional personal sales OK?” |
| Trade-in car / electronics | Personal | Usually fine |
| Consign clothes you own | Personal | Fine if not a resale business |

**Kill criterion:** If you’re buying to resell, that’s a business → likely still “no.”

### Bucket C — Truly passive royalties / ads on assets you already use
| Idea | Notes | Confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car wrap advertising (Wrapify-style) | You drive anyway; company pays for ad space | Is this outside business / advertising income restricted? |
| Bank / credit card / referral bonuses for **personal** use | Not a hustle farm | Referral program rules; no soliciting coworkers |
| Credit card points optimization | Personal finance | Usually OK |

### Bucket D — Rent idle personal property (often “outside business”)
These are popular and automated — **but Compliance may still say no** because they generate business income:

| Idea | Automation | Likely Compliance stance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neighbor.com driveway / garage / shed | High | Ask explicitly |
| Occasional game-day parking | Medium | Ask (can look commercial) |
| Turo | Medium | Often denied |
| Airbnb spare room | Low–medium | Often denied / permit + OBA |
| PeerSpace room rental | Medium | Ask |

**Do not list these until you get a written yes for “renting personal property / unused space.”**

### Bucket E — Digital creations with no clients
| Idea | Automation | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Print on demand (no inventory) | High | Selling goods online = business → ask |
| Gumroad / Etsy digital downloads | High | Same |
| KDP ebook / low-content | High | Same |
| YouTube / blog with ads/affiliates | Medium | Media business / endorsements → ask (esp. financials/security topics) |
| Affiliate links | Medium | Endorsements / vendor conflicts |

Security, banking, WF, or vendor content is higher risk. Non-work hobbies (gardening, cooking, Ohio travel, gaming) are easier to defend if allowed at all.

### Bucket F — Pure hobby with incidental income
| Idea | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Haunt/volunteer season (Pataskala history) unpaid or club reimbursement | Confirm if any paid gig work is banned |
| Creative hobbies sold rarely (art, crafts) | “Occasional hobby sales” vs business |

## Practical path if the answer stays “no” to businesses

If Compliance blocks **all** income-generating outside activity:

1. **Maximize W-2** — promotions, certs WF pays for, internal mobility (encryption → broader IAM/sec eng).  
2. **Maximize benefits** — 401k match, HSA, ESPP if available, emergency fund in HYSA.  
3. **Cut costs** — biggest “return” with zero Compliance risk.  
4. **Skills for later** — build labs/portfolio off-hours with **no monetization** until you leave or get approval.  
5. **Revisit when role changes** — new team or employer may allow OBAs.

Automated wealth building that usually survives a hard “no” to side businesses: **save/invest**, not “hustle.”

## Script to send Compliance (copy/paste)

> Compliance previously advised I may not pursue outside employment. Before I drop everything, can you confirm whether any of the following are permitted without an OBA, or require disclosure?
>
> 1. Personal investing in public securities / funds (no advisory to others)  
> 2. Occasional sale of my own used household items  
> 3. Renting unused personal driveway/garage space via a peer marketplace  
> 4. Print-on-demand or digital downloads with no client work and no use of WF IP/time/systems  
> 5. Vehicle wrap advertising while commuting  
> 6. Ad-supported hobby content unrelated to banking or cybersecurity  
>
> I will not start any of these until I have written guidance. I will not use Wells Fargo time, systems, data, or vendors.

## If they only banned “employment” but allow passive

Then the investigation order is:

1. Get written OK on **Bucket A** (always clarify restricted list).  
2. Get written OK on **Bucket C** car wrap.  
3. Get written OK on **Bucket D** Neighbor parking/storage — best local automated fit in Columbus if allowed.  
4. Get written OK on **Bucket E** POD/digital — only non-work niches.  
5. Ignore all service/gig/consulting ideas permanently while at WF.

---

## Bottom line

Compliance “no” means **stop building businesses and client work**. Next move is a **category clarification email**, not a new hustle list. The only reliably safe “automation” without approval is usually **personal investing + frugality**; everything that earns marketplace income needs an explicit green light.
