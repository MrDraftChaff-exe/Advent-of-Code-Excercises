## Employer & ethics checklist (non-negotiable)

1. Read current Wells Fargo Code of Conduct / outside business activity process; **pre-clear before any consulting**.
2. Never use WF data, diagrams, runbooks, vendor portals, or internal tooling.
3. Prefer **education for individuals** and **generic SMB templates** over bank/enterprise PAM implementations.
4. Avoid naming clients; no GuidePoint/WF war stories that identify systems.
5. Watch CyberArk trademark and certification logo usage rules for marketing.
6. Separate devices/accounts; no side hustle during work hours or on corporate equipment.
7. Track Ohio/self-employment taxes if revenue appears.
