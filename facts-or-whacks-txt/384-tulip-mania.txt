384. Tulip Mania
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Semper_Augustus_tulip.jpg

Hook: One bulb. Price of a HOUSE.

Dutch tulip bubble 1636-1637.
Single tulip bulb cost more than a house.
First recorded speculative bubble.
Market crashed February 1637 overnight.
Became cautionary tale of greed.
