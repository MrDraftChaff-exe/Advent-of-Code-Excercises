208. Easter Rising 1916
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Easter_Rising_1916.jpg

Hook: Dublin. 6 days. IRELAND changed.

Irish republicans seized Dublin April 1916.
Held out six days against British forces.
Leaders executed including Padraig Pearse.
Executions turned public opinion to independence.
Led to Irish Free State 1922.
