31. Building the Great Pyramids
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e3/Kheops-Pyramid.jpg

Hook: 2.3 million blocks. No cranes.

Built circa 2560 BCE for Pharaoh Khufu.
Each block weighed roughly 2.5 tons.
Workforce likely paid laborers not slaves.
Aligned to cardinal points with stunning precision.
Still among the largest structures ever built.
