Hook: Tax without representation. Colonists EXPLODED.

British Parliament passed Stamp Act 1765.
Tax on every printed document in colonies.
Colonists cried taxation without representation.
Sons of Liberty organized boycotts and protests.
Repealed 1766 but damage to trust was done.
