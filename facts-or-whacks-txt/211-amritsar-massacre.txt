211. Amritsar Massacre
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Jallianwala_Bagh.jpg

Hook: 379 killed. Empire EXPOSED.

British troops fired on crowd April 13 1919.
Unarmed Indians gathered in Jallianwala Bagh.
379 killed over 1,200 wounded.
General Dyer ordered firing without warning.
Turning point in Indian independence movement.
