48. Stonehenge Mystery
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/3c/Stonehenge2007_07_30.jpg

Hook: Built before Egypt's pyramids. WHY?

Constructed 3000-2000 BCE on Salisbury Plain.
Some stones dragged 150 miles from Wales.
Aligned with summer and winter solstices.
Purpose debated: temple calendar or burial site.
Built by people without metal tools or wheels.
