336. Stonewall Riots
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Stonewall_Inn_1969.jpg

Hook: Riots that started a MOVEMENT.

NYPD raided Stonewall Inn June 28 1969.
Patrons fought back against police harassment.
Six days of protests followed.
Birth of modern LGBTQ+ rights movement.
Pride Month commemorates Stonewall.
