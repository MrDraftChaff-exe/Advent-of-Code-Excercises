32. Hammurabi's Code
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0e/Code_of_Hammurabi%2C_Louvre.jpg

Hook: 282 laws. Eye for an EYE.

Carved on a 7-foot stone stele circa 1754 BCE.
One of the oldest written legal codes — a key part of the story of Hammurabi's Code.
Punishments scaled by social class — a key part of the story of Hammurabi's Code.
Famous principle: eye for an eye — a key part of the story of Hammurabi's Code.
Displayed publicly so no one could claim ignorance.
Hammurabi was the sixth king of Babylon and ruled from approximately 1792 to 1750 BCE.
The code included detailed laws on trade, family, property, and criminal punishment.
Its principle of proportional justice influenced legal systems across the ancient Near East.
