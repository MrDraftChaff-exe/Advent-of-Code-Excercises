66. Nazca Lines
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5a/Nazca-monkey.jpg

Hook: Giant drawings. Only visible from SKY.

Created 500 BCE to 500 CE in Peru desert.
Include monkey spider hummingbird and whale.
Lines up to 30 miles long.
Purpose unknown: astronomy rituals or water.
Only fully visible from aircraft altitude.
