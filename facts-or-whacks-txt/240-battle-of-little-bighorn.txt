240. Battle of Little Bighorn
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Little_Bighorn_battlefield.jpg

Hook: Custer's last STAND.

June 25-26 1876 Montana Territory.
Lakota and Cheyenne defeated Custer's 7th Cavalry.
Custer and all 210 men killed.
Greatest Native American victory over US Army.
Led to intensified campaigns against Plains tribes.
