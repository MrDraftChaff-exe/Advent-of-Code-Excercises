296. Concorde Supersonic Jet
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Concorde_on_approach_to_JFK.jpg

Hook: Mach 2. Champagne at 60,000 FEET.

First supersonic passenger jet entered service 1976.
New York to London in 3.5 hours.
Flew at twice the speed of sound.
Retired 2003 after crash and economics.
Never replaced in commercial aviation.
