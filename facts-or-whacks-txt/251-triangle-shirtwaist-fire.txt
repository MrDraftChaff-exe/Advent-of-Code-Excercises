251. Triangle Shirtwaist Fire
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Triangle_Shirtwaist_Factory_fire.jpg

Hook: 146 workers. Doors LOCKED.

Factory fire March 25 1911 New York City.
146 garment workers mostly young women died.
Exit doors locked to prevent theft.
Jumped from windows to escape flames.
Sparked labor reform and fire safety laws.
