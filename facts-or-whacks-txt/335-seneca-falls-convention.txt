Hook: 68 women. 32 men. HISTORY.

First women's rights convention July 1848.
Elizabeth Cady Stanton wrote Declaration of Sentiments.
Demanded vote property rights and education.
Only one attendee lived to see 19th Amendment.
Birth of organized women's rights movement.
