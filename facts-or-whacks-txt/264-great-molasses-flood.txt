264. Great Molasses Flood
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Molasses_disaster.jpg

Hook: 15 feet of MOLASSES.

January 15 1919 Boston.
Storage tank burst releasing 2.3 million gallons.
Wave 15 feet high 35 mph through streets.
21 killed 150 injured.
Sticky cleanup took months.
