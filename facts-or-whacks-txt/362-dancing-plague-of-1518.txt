362. Dancing Plague of 1518
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Dancing_plague_1518.jpg

Hook: Danced until they DIED.

Strasbourg July 1518 woman started dancing.
Up to 400 joined dancing for days.
Some died from exhaustion stroke or heart attack.
Cause unknown mass hysteria or ergot poisoning.
One of history's strangest events.
