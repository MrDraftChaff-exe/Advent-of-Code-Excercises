52. Phoenician Alphabet
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/07/Phoenician_alphabet.svg

Hook: 22 letters. You still use THEM.

Developed circa 1050 BCE in modern Lebanon.
First widely used phonetic alphabet.
Spread via Phoenician maritime trade network.
Ancestor of Greek Latin and Arabic scripts.
Made literacy accessible beyond scribes.
