149. Suez Canal
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Suez_Canal%2C_Egypt.jpg

Hook: 120 miles. Britain RULED trade.

Opened 1869 connecting Mediterranean to Red Sea.
Built by Ferdinand de Lesseps and Egyptian labor.
Britain bought controlling share 1875.
Nationalized by Nasser 1956 sparking crisis.
Still vital chokepoint for world trade.
