33. Battle of Thermopylae
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Leonidas_at_Thermopylae.jpg

Hook: 300 Spartans. One million Persians.

Fought August 480 BCE at a narrow mountain pass.
King Leonidas led roughly 300 Spartans.
Held off Xerxes' massive Persian army for three days.
Betrayed when a local showed Persians a secret path.
Became the ultimate symbol of heroic last stands.
