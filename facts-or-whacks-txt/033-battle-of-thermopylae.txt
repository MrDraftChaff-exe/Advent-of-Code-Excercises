33. Battle of Thermopylae
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9a/Leonidas_at_Thermopylae.jpg

Hook: 300 Spartans. One million Persians.

Fought August 480 BCE at a narrow mountain pass.
King Leonidas led roughly 300 Spartans — a key part of the story of Battle of Thermopylae.
Held off Xerxes' massive Persian army for three days.
Betrayed when a local showed Persians a secret path.
Became the ultimate symbol of heroic last stands.
The Spartan stand at Thermopylae bought critical time for Greece to organize its defenses.
The Greek historian Herodotus recorded the battle, blending history with enduring legend.
A monument at the site honors the Spartans who obeyed their laws and stayed to fight.
