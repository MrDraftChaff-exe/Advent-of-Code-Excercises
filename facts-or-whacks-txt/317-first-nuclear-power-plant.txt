Hook: Atoms for PEACE.

Obninsk USSR 1954 first grid-connected plant.
Calder Hall UK 1956 first commercial plant.
Promised unlimited clean energy.
Chernobyl and Fukushima changed perception.
Still provides 10 percent of world electricity.
