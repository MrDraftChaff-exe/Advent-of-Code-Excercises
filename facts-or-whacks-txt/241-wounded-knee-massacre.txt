241. Wounded Knee Massacre
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Wounded_Knee_massacre.jpg

Hook: 300 killed. Ghost Dance ENDED.

December 29 1890 South Dakota.
US cavalry killed 300 Lakota men women children.
Last major conflict of Indian Wars.
Occurred during Ghost Dance religious movement.
Medals of Honor awarded to soldiers later questioned.
