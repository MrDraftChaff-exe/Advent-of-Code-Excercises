119. Salem Witch Trials
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/8a/Witchcraft_at_Salem_Village.jpg

Hook: 20 hanged. Mass HYSTERIA.

Occurred 1692-1693 in Massachusetts colony.
Over 200 accused mostly women.
20 executed by hanging one pressed to death.
Spectral evidence allowed in court.
Led to reforms in American legal standards.
