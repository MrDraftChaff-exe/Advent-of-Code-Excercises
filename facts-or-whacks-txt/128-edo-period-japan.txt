Hook: 250 years of PEACE.

Tokugawa shogunate ruled 1603-1868.
Sakoku policy closed Japan to foreigners.
Samurai class and urban culture flourished.
Kabuki ukiyo-e and haiku arts peaked.
Ended when Perry's Black Ships arrived 1853.
