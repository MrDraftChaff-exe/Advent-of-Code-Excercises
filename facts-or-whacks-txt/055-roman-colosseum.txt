55. Roman Colosseum
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/de/Colosseo_2020.jpg

Hook: 50,000 spectators. Death as SPORT.

Completed 80 CE under Emperor Titus.
Hosted gladiator fights and mock sea battles.
Free admission to Roman citizens via bread and circuses.
Estimated 400,000 died in arena over centuries.
Still stands as icon of Roman engineering.
