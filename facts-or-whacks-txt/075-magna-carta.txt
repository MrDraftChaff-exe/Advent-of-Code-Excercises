75. Magna Carta
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/5e/Magna_Carta_%28British_Library_Cotton_MS_Augustus_II.106%29.jpg

Hook: A king forced to SIGN his power away.

Signed June 15 1215 at Runnymede England.
King John forced by rebellious barons.
Limited royal power and protected feudal rights.
Foundation of constitutional law worldwide.
Only three clauses still valid in UK law.
