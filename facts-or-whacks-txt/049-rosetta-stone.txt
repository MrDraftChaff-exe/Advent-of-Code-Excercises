49. Rosetta Stone
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/23/Rosetta_Stone.JPG

Hook: One rock. Egypt UNLOCKED.

Discovered 1799 by French soldiers in Egypt.
Same decree in three scripts: hieroglyphics demotic Greek.
Jean-Francois Champollion cracked code 1822.
Opened 3,000 years of Egyptian history.
Now displayed at British Museum London.
