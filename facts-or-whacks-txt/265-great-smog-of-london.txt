265. Great Smog of London
Image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4e/Great_Smog_of_London.jpg

Hook: 5 days. 12,000 DEAD.

December 1952 coal smoke trapped over London.
Visibility dropped to a few feet.
Estimated 12,000 died from respiratory failure.
Led to Clean Air Act 1956.
Changed environmental policy worldwide.
