# Weekend in Columbus — 48-Hour Field Kit

A tight plan for visitors (or locals hosting them). Pick a pace, follow the skeleton, swap freely.

## How to use

1. Choose **Pace A** (walkable / food-forward) or **Pace B** (mix of city + one outing).
2. Check weather Friday night. Use the swap list if storms or extreme heat/cold.
3. Park once per neighborhood when you can. Rideshare between clusters at night.

### Saturday

- Morning: North Market + downtown walk
- Midday: drive or rideshare to **Hocking Hills** *or* **Ohio Caverns** *or* a Metro Park hike (pick based on drive time)

## Neighborhood pairing guide

| Pair | Why it works | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short North → German Village | Different energy, easy story for guests | Trying to “do everything” on High St twice |
| Clintonville → Scioto | Calm morning, open-air afternoon | Rush-hour I-71 frustration without a buffer |
| Easton → hotel reset | Low-friction for families | Expecting walkable historic charm |

---

## Weather-flex swaps

- **Heavy rain:** museums, Book Loft, North Market, mall corridors at Easton
- **Heat advisory:** shade walks early, indoor afternoon, patio dinner only after 7pm
- **Snow / ice:** cut the outing; keep one neighborhood walking radius
