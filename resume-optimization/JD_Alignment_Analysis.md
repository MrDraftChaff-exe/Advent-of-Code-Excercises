# Job Description Alignment Analysis
## Target Role: Veterinary Assistant / Veterinary Technician — Sunbury Veterinary Clinic (Sunbury, OH)

### Top Required Skills & Competencies
1. **Patient care / nursing support** — assisting with clinical care of animals
2. **Laboratory diagnostics** — sample handling and diagnostic support
3. **Surgical and medical procedure support** — assisting veterinarians during procedures
4. **Client communication and education** — clear, compassionate interaction with pet owners
5. **Companion and exotic animal handling** — comfort with diverse species
6. **Dependability and strong work ethic** — reliable, enthusiastic team membership
7. **Desire to learn / trainability** — openness to mentoring and hands-on training
8. **Compassionate, supportive teamwork** — fit for a family-oriented clinical culture

### Key Responsibilities & Priorities
- Support veterinary hospital operations in full-time or part-time capacity
- Assist with patient care, nursing, surgical/medical procedures, and laboratory diagnostics
- Communicate with and educate clients
- Contribute to a positive, supportive, family-oriented workplace
- Grow through mentorship and hands-on training with experienced veterinarians and staff

### Repeated Keywords & Phrases
- Veterinary Assistant / Veterinary Technician
- Laboratory diagnostics
- Patient care / nursing
- Surgical and medical procedures
- Client communication / client education
- Companion and exotic animals
- Mentoring / hands-on training / desire to learn
- Dependable, enthusiastic, compassionate
- Family-owned / supportive environment
- Competitive compensation, retirement, paid vacation/holidays

### Seniority Level & Scope Expectations
- **Entry to early-career support roles** (Assistant and Technician openings)
- Prior animal experience is **preferred, not required**
- Explicitly welcomes candidates beginning their journey who demonstrate love of animals, strong work ethic, and desire to learn
- Mentorship culture for pre-veterinary and veterinary-interested candidates
- Scope centers on clinical support, lab work, procedures, and client-facing service rather than independent veterinary practice

### Resume Prioritization Guidance (from this analysis)
1. Lead with **American Red Cross Collections Tech** experience (sample collection, safety protocols, laboratory equipment, documentation) as the strongest clinical/lab-adjacent alignment.
2. Emphasize **client/customer communication**, accuracy, and service quality from retail roles.
3. Surface **reliability, teamwork, flexible scheduling (nights/weekends/holidays), sanitation, and inventory/organization** as transferable clinic-support strengths.
4. Do **not** claim animal care, veterinary procedures, exotic species work, or clinical nursing experience not present in the source resume.
